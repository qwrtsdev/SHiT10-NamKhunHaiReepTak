How to scoop
  1. Pair the Joy-Con in your OS Bluetooth settings (hold the sync button on the rail), then press Connect.
  2. Dip: point the top of the Joy-Con (the shoulder-button end) down. The bucket fills; you feel it glug.
  3. Scoop: flip it up. Water leaves every tab, and you feel the splash.
  4. Lift too slowly (over 1.5 s) and it spills back.

Needs Chrome/Edge on desktop (WebHID). Joy-Con L, Joy-Con R and the Pro Controller work; you can use two at once.
